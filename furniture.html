The Work of Things

A place for objects that can still carry weight, memory and use.

This territory follows furniture through observation, dismantling, repair and return. It begins with the thing itself: its joints, wear, failures and the decisions that let it continue without pretending it has become new.

Enter the first object ↗
STRUCTURAL STUDYWing chair

These are structural studies, not catalogued works. They show how an object holds together.

01 / THE METHOD

The work starts before the repair.

  1. Observe

    Read material, construction, wear and risk before deciding what the object needs.

    01
  2. Open

    Remove only what must be removed. The concealed structure is evidence, not waste.

    02
  3. Repair

    Restore strength without disguising a structural failure as a cosmetic improvement.

    03
  4. Return

    The work ends in ordinary use: a chair carries a body; a drawer opens; a table stands.

    04

02 / THE BOUNDARY
Repair does not erase time. It makes continued use possible.

What this page isA developing public record of material practice, object studies and completed work.

What it is notIt is not an official workshop record, a catalogue of unverified provenance, or a claim that every old object should be saved.

What comes nextNew objects enter only when their condition, process and result can be documented honestly.
