Synchronicity & Serendipity

Twenty-one documented chains of coincidence, drawn as one moving field. Dreams, paintings, a street corner in Dublin and the people through whom the chains passed. Time winds outwards from the centre.

One field, many chains

Each star is one chain: several coincidences joined by one meaning within a short span of time. Its colour shows where the chain began, in a dream, in a waking image or thought, or in an event outside. Its size and the small moons around it show how many linked coincidences it holds. The diamonds are images that keep returning, the squares are places, the circles are people and mediators. Golden threads mark a direct continuation, where one chain leads straight into the next.

The words used here

The types follow the psychiatrist and coincidence researcher Bernard Beitman, Connecting with Coincidence (2016), Meaningful Coincidences (2022) and Life-Changing Synchronicities (2025). One chain often carries several types at once.

Siquilizm

Siquilizm is the working term for what the field shows as a whole. Unlike events arrange themselves over time into one sequence of meaning. Each new link continues the meaning of the one before, though it comes from another part of life: a dream, a painting, a street, a book, a person. From inside it is experienced as being led. It differs from seriality, which rests on the repetition of the same thing, a name or a number, and from a single synchronicity, which joins one inner state to one outer event. Siquilizm describes the chain of such moments and how one leads to the next.

What the field does not prove

The field counts what was remembered and written down. It cannot show the days when nothing happened, or images that led nowhere. Several links passed through people who share the same interests, or through AI systems that answer what they are asked; they are marked as mediated, because they are less independent than the rest. The strongest links are those where my own part was smallest: the man from Riga standing on the spot photographed the day before, a death on Warhol's date, the duck on the roof. A log of misses, kept from now on, is the only honest answer to the question of selection.

Privacy

Private people appear by first name only, and some only by their relation to me. There are no photographs of private people's faces: the images are my own paintings, drawings and objects, film stills made with AI, and public pages. Health, faith and other sensitive details of other people are left out. Scholars, artists and public figures are named in full where their public work is part of the story. If you appear here and would like something changed or removed, write to me and I will do it.
