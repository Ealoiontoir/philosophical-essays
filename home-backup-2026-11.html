Ealoiontoir

Aivar Drukalski · Artist

Born of Baltic roots, based in Ireland. Painting is where I begin: with images, dreams, memory, and what the hand discovers before an explanation arrives.

What can AI understand about a life — and what must remain in human hands?

Baltic roots. A life and artistic practice in Ireland.
I work with painting, memory and AI dialogue to explore how a life becomes an archive — and how an archive changes the way we see.

A HUMAN–AI EXPERIMENT

One painting. Three ways of looking.

Can an archive keep
the uncertainty alive?

A painting has a surface. It also has a history. Open the layers below to see the question behind this practice: how can AI help connect a life without deciding its meaning for the person who lived it?

01 / THE OBJECTLook before interpreting +

Start with the portrait and its reverse. Look at the visible marks before reading the story attached to them.

TURN THE CANVAS ↓
02 / THE ACCOUNTLet the person speak +

A first-person account adds memory and context. It belongs beside the object, with its own uncertainties. A remembered event and a material trace can tell different parts of a story.

EXPLORE THE ACCOUNTS ↓
03 / THE INTERPRETATIONKeep the connection open +

AI dialogue can suggest comparisons and questions. The connection still needs to be checked against the source, and the author can reject it. An elegant explanation is an invitation to examine the evidence.

ENTER THE CODEX ↓

A question for people building AI.

How would you design a system that lets a person revisit, challenge and revise what it says about their life?

This artistic practice offers a starting point for a conversation about personal archives, human judgment and the design of AI memory.

01 / ORIGIN

Intuition as instrument. Painting as passage.

I do not begin
with an explanation.

I think of myself as a listener — to dreams, memory, images that arrive uninvited, and the strange pressure that asks for form.

“I never set out to create a style. I follow what pulls. There is no program. Only presence.”

05 / LIBER NOVUS

Approach.
Step back.
Return changed.

I approach it. I step back. Only later did I understand — this is the correct rhythm. The pauses are part of the reading. Jung himself took sixteen years.

Not a shortcut into symbolic certainty. A model of how descent requires time.
06 / THE CODEX

A trace of thinking becoming form.

Not a diary.
Not a database.
An organ of discernment.

Four years of paintings, dreams, emails, letters, political fragments and almost two thousand core AI dialogues accumulated without a plan. The archive began to reproduce the conditions under which understanding happens.
