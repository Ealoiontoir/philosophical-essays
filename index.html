Before the first chapter

A life that slowly
became an archive

My name is Aivar Drukalski. I paint under the name Ealoiontoir. Born in Latvia. Living and painting in Ireland.
This site is my experiment. It began with paintings and with coincidences I could not explain. It grew into an archive of images, dreams, letters and conversations. Now it is moving toward a book.
I do not know how it ends. That is part of the experiment.

IA place

A place
that let me in.

Some places stay foreign for years. Then one day you understand you are home.
In Ireland something in me began to work. Images arrived. Meetings and coincidences came one after another.
I would never have found them in Latvia.
They were
waiting here.
IIHow I paint

I follow
what pulls.

I do not plan what I paint. An image arrives first. Understanding comes later, sometimes years later.
There are long quiet periods. I read, I walk, I wait. Then everything comes together at once. I have learned to trust both.
I paint in oil on linen. I also work with cardboard, wax, charcoal, engraved aluminium and found objects. Every painting has a front and a back. The back keeps dates, notes and traces. Both belong to the work.

IIIA thread of coincidences

First the event.
The names came later.

Around 2021 things began to answer each other. A face, a dream, a street, a book, a building. I did not have words for it yet. I started writing one word in my notebook. Coincidence.
Jung called this synchronicity. I learned his word only later. Below is the thread as it happened to me. Each step shows the event and, separately, what I understood afterwards.
NCAD · Dublin

Gerard

A man in Dublin. His face, his voice, his words about rain, wind, birds and waking in the morning. I worked with him through photographs, cardboard, wax and paint.
LaterHe became the first living voice of the archive.
October 2021

The dream

An Alpine room. A hidden door. Darkness. Two unknown elders. The left hand. A slow rotation. No answer. I drew it so I would not lose it.
LaterIt became the painting The Hidden Door and the story The Threshold.
29 – 30 November 2021

Dorset Street

Two mornings on the same Dublin street. A man on the steps. The next day, a connection with my earlier life in Riga. I began to write the word Coincidence in my notebook.
LaterI could describe the surprise. I could not yet explain it.
NCAD

Someone listens

I told my teacher and curator Beth O'Halloran about these coincidences. She suggested I read about Hilma af Klint. She also told me to draw the dream freely. A church, a lake and mountains appeared on the paper.
LaterOne small act of listening opened years of reading.
The theatre

The studio from the dream

In Red, a play about Mark Rothko, I recognised the studio I had seen in my dream.
LaterI learned that Rothko was born in Daugavpils, in Latvia, not far from where I come from.
Switzerland

The place I had already found

Searching the map for the place in my drawings, I found a building. I did not know its name. Following Hilma af Klint to Rudolf Steiner, I arrived at the same building again. The Goetheanum.
LaterThe first time I found it by looking. The second time by a name.
Afterwards

The beacons

Then came the people who had walked here before me. Goethe, Steiner, Jung, Rothko, Hilma af Klint, Arendt, Tarkovsky and others.
LaterNot a pantheon. A sequence of arrivals.

IVA conversation with machines

The machines connect.
I decide.

My work with AI began in 2022 and continues to develop as the practice I call Quiet Collaboration.
The Codex brings together paintings, dreams, letters, political notes and the dialogues themselves. It was never planned as a finished collection. It continues to grow with the work.
The machines help me see connections. They do not decide what my life means. That stays with the person who lived it.

VSpirit of Time, today

Where psyche,
memory and power meet

Spirit of Time is the name of the whole project. It holds two clocks. The outer one is history, politics and the speed of our age. The inner one is dream, memory and what cannot be hurried.
Today I work on it in public. On Instagram I place my own works next to imagined conversations with artists I learn from. The dialogue is my own writing and it is clearly fiction. The question is always about attention. What do we still notice?
Franz Kafkabeside Vise, the realities of our timeWe saved the thought, then made it impossible to read.
Eva Hessebeside the painting CarePerfectly straight, according to whom?
Barbara Hepworthbeside a winter bridge sketchWe added another connection. What still stands between us?
I also write about history and power, about the war and about the Baltic. I see this as witnessing. The archive has to face the dark of its own time.

VIToward a book

From private chaos
to a form others can carry

The aim is simple to say and hard to do. To turn a life full of images and coincidences into something another person can walk through.
The paintings came first. Then the archive. Now the book. I am not trying to prove anything. I want to keep the questions alive and honest.
In progress
The Odyssey of the Vitruvian Odysseus
One of the projects being written now, in its own way and at its own pace. A journey still under way. It is being born, not finished.
