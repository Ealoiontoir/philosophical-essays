The Field of Coincidences

A fractal map of twenty-two documented coincidence nodes, 2019 to 2026. Each node is itself a small chain. The chains are linked by shared places, recurring images and the people through whom they passed. Seen together, they lie in one field of meaning.

How to read the map

Every coloured point is one node, a group of coincidences joined by one meaning within a short span of time. Its colour shows where it began. Its size and the small dots around it show how many linked coincidences it holds. Three or more makes a multiplier. A dotted halo means the chain came back to a place already marked.

The outlined shapes are the threads that run between nodes. Diamonds are carrier images, the motifs that pass from one chain to the next, such as the Flammarion engraving, the elephant and the hidden room. Squares are places. Circles are people and mediators. Solid lines mark a direct continuation, where one node leads straight into another.

The Timeline view lays the same points along the years. The Field view lets them settle by their connections. In that view the pattern repeats at every scale. A single coincidence is a link. Several links make a node. Nodes cluster around a carrier image, and the clusters hang together in one field. This repetition is the fractal in the title.

The four types, after Beitman

Siquilizm

Siquilizm (siquilizm) is the working term for what the map shows as a whole. Unlike events arrange themselves over time into one sequence inside a single field of meaning, mental and semantic. Each new link continues the meaning of the one before, though it comes from another part of life: a dream, a painting, a street, a book, a person. From inside, it is experienced as being led.

It differs from seriality, which rests on the repetition of the same thing, a name or a number. Siquilizm rests on the continuation of meaning, while the form of the links can be entirely different. It also differs from a single synchronicity, which joins one inner state to one outer event. Siquilizm describes the chain of such moments and how one leads to the next.

What the map does not prove

The map counts what was remembered and written down. It cannot show the days when nothing happened, and it cannot show images that led nowhere. Several links passed through people who share my interests, or through AI systems that answer what they are asked. These are marked as mediated in each node, because they are less independent than the rest. The strongest links are those where my own part was smallest: the customer from Riga standing on the spot of a photograph taken the day before, a death on Warhol's date, the duck on the roof. A log of misses, kept from now on, is the only honest answer to the question of selection.
