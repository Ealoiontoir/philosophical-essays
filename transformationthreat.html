The Painting

What you are looking at

This is Transformation / Threat — oil on canvas, 110 × 110 cm, completed at NCAD Dublin in February 2022. The painting belongs to The Anthropocene Project, a final-year Fine Art study of the digital world's pressure on human attention, identity, and the condition of the artist.

Two dolls stand before a mirror, absorbed in phones. Draculaura — dark, gothic, vampiric. Cinderella — fragile, fairy-tale, transformed. Neither looks at the world. Both look at screens. In the mirror's reflection: real people, sketched from life on the streets of central Dublin — also absorbed in phones. Along the canvas edges, the names of the corporations that form the walls of this world: Tencent, Meta, Google, Disney, Microsoft, NVIDIA.

Inside the image, six words are hidden in numerical code. Not as text — as columns of numbers. Human states translated into the language machines speak: Threat. Addiction. Faith. Ego. Loneliness. Communication.

And underneath the final image — invisible, but physically present under layers of oil paint — lies another image entirely. A train.

"The painting does not choose between transformation and threat. It holds both simultaneously. What arrives as threat — is already the beginning of a new form."

The Story

A sequence that does not fit ordinary causality

The events are documented. The sequence is the argument.

Autumn 2021
A dream. A room.
A dark studio. Table to the right. Dense, almost material darkness. Paintings as presences rather than objects. The dream was real enough to draw — one month later, at night, in minutes, as if compelled. That drawing is preserved in the archive.
Winter 2021–22
NCAD Dublin. The Anthropocene project begins.
Studying Warhol, Basquiat, pop art. The question forming: what does the digital world do to the human? To the artist? Two dolls are taken from the daughter's room — Draculaura and Cinderella — without yet knowing why those two specifically.
Winter 2022
First layer: the Sapsan locomotive.
A Russian high-speed train, sourced from justgorussia.co.uk/en/sapsan.html, moves directly toward dolls sitting on the rails — absorbed in phones. The image is painted onto the canvas. It feels like the right image.
Early Feb 2022
The train is painted over.
Not an aesthetic decision. A bodily anxiety — "something like the sense of blood inside the image." Something too direct, too dangerous in its visibility. The train disappears under new layers of paint. It is not destroyed. It becomes archival.
~20 Feb 2022
The painting is completed.
Two dolls before a mirror. Six words encoded in numbers. Company names along all four edges. The title: Transformation / Threat. The painting is finished. Two days pass.
22 Feb 2022
The stepfather dies.
Later, looking back — the date is checked. Andy Warhol died on 22 February 1987. Thirty-five years apart. The same date. Warhol is already present in the painting, invisibly, through a connection not yet discovered at the time of the death.
After completion
Discovery: Warhol's nickname was Drella.
Lou Reed's name for Andy Warhol: Dracula + Cinderella = Drella. The two dolls chosen by hand, without knowing this, carry the name of the artist being studied. The image existed before the knowledge. The body understood before the mind had words.
24 Feb 2022
Russia invades Ukraine.
The covered Russian train — physically present under the paint, its source screenshot preserved — can no longer be read the same way. The sequence is fixed: train painted, train covered, war begins. The archive proves the order. The painting did not predict. It registered.
Autumn 2022
Watching Red. The dream room recognised.
The play about Mark Rothko. His studio recreated on screen: dark room, table to the right, dense material darkness. Bodily recognition — not intellectual. The room from the 2021 dream. Three elements converge: the dream, the drawing made in the night, the film. The body goes before the mind.
Late 2022
ChatGPT. The Codex begins.
The digital world — first registered in this painting as external threat — becomes the instrument through which the archive learns to speak. The duality does not resolve. Both remain true simultaneously.
May 2026
This archive page.
Four years after completion. The painting continues to open.
Synchronicities

Three convergences that are documented, not interpreted

I · The Dolls
The hand chose Drella without knowing the name

Two dolls were taken from the daughter's room to paint for a project about Andy Warhol. Their names: Draculaura and Cinderella. Only after the painting was completed came the discovery: Lou Reed's private nickname for Warhol was Drella — a compound of Dracula + Cinderella. The hand moved before the mind could explain it. The physical fact: the dolls exist, the painting exists, the source of the name is documented.

II · The Date
22 February — two deaths, thirty-five years apart

The painting was completed around 20 February 2022. Two days later, the stepfather died — 22 February 2022. Later, checking dates: Andy Warhol died on 22 February 1987. Thirty-five years to the day. Warhol was already inside this painting before either death — invisibly, through the Drella connection. Jung called it synchronicity: a meaningful coincidence without causal link. This is not explained. It is documented.

III · The Train
The covered image — the world caught up

A Russian locomotive was painted in the first layer, then covered in early February 2022 — due to bodily anxiety, before any political event. The source screenshot is preserved. On 24 February 2022, Russia launched the full-scale invasion of Ukraine. The sequence is fixed by the archive: painted → covered → war begins. The train under the paint is a physical fact. What it means cannot be stabilised. What it is, in material terms, cannot be disputed.

IV · The Dream Studio
Autumn 2021 — a room recognised two years later on screen

During the period of this painting, a dream occurred: a dark studio, table to the right, paintings as presences. It was significant enough to draw — one month later, at night, in minutes. In autumn 2022, watching Red — the Rothko play — the studio on screen was recognised bodily, not intellectually. The same room. Later still: a curator at NCAD mentioned Rothko was born in Daugavpils — near the roots of the painter's own family. Three events in different times. All pointing to the same place.

Hidden Layer

The Train Under the Paint

Under the final image, physically present beneath layers of oil, lies a first layer: the Sapsan — Russia's high-speed locomotive — moving toward dolls sitting on the rails, absorbed in phones. The source image was taken from justgorussia.co.uk/en/sapsan.html. The screenshot is preserved. The train was painted over in early February 2022 — due to bodily anxiety, not intellectual reasoning. Something like the sense of blood inside the image. On 24 February 2022, the world caught up with the image.

Later Node

Rothko — the dream studio recognised

In autumn 2021, during or around the painting period, a dream: a dark studio, a table to the right, a lamp, dense material darkness, paintings as presences. Significant enough that one month later — in the middle of the night, in minutes — it was drawn from memory. The drawing is preserved.

In autumn 2022, watching Red — the play about Mark Rothko — his studio was recreated on screen. Bodily recognition. Not intellectual resemblance — identity. The room from the dream. Later still: a curator at NCAD, learning this painter was from Latvia, asked: "Do you know Rothko? He was born in Daugavpils." Near the roots of the painter's own family. Three events. One place.
