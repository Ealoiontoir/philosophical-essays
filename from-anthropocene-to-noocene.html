From Anthropocene
to Noocene

The Hidden Threshold of Transformation / Threat

"Before I knew the name of the force,
I painted its pressure."

May 29, 2026 · Dublin · A response to Mikhail Epstein

"If the Anthropocene revealed how human ego became a geological force — the Noocene poses the question that epoch cannot answer: can reason exit the ego that is destroying the world?"

In response to Mikhail Epstein · "Archaeology" interview · Radio Svoboda · May 2026

The First Document

Transformation / Threat — A Painting at the Threshold

In 2021–2022, at NCAD Dublin, within the Anthropocene project, a painting was made. Oil on canvas. 110 × 110 cm. It was completed before the Noocene had a name. Before AI became the mass face of the digital world. Before I could articulate what it registered. The image preceded the concept.

Visible Layer
Final painting · around 20 February 2022
Hidden Layer
Sapsan locomotive · first layer · painted over

Two dolls before a mirror. Draculaura and Cinderella — dark and light. Both absorbed in phones. In the mirror: life-sketches of real Dubliners, also in their screens. Around the edges of the canvas: Tencent, Meta, Google, Disney, Microsoft, NVIDIA. Not as critique — as the walls of the room. The infrastructure that existed before the figures arrived inside it.

Six words encoded in columns of numbers: THREAT, ADDICTION, FAITH, EGO, LONELINESS, COMMUNICATION. Human states translated into the language machines speak. The word EGO was embedded in the centre — before I knew it was the centre of the question.

I

The Anthropocene: An Internal Reading

The Anthropocene is not only a geological term. It is the epoch in which the human species, through the amplifying force of its technology, began rewriting the conditions of life on Earth. That much is well-established science.

But there is a deeper reading. The Anthropocene is the epoch in which biological ego — scaled to industrial and digital magnitude — became a planetary force. Not the human as a species, exactly. The human as a creature driven by hunger, fear of death, the need to dominate, to possess, to prove its own exceptional status, to convert its trauma into ideology.

The word EGO — already there

When I encoded six words into the painting's numerical columns, EGO was among them. УГРОЗА. ЗАВИСИМОСТЬ. ВЕРА. ЭГО. ОДИНОЧЕСТВО. ОБЩЕНИЕ. Threat. Addiction. Faith. Ego. Loneliness. Communication. The word stood at the centre of the painting's diagnosis before I was conscious it was the diagnosis.

The FRAGILE sheet

A working archive sheet, labelled FRAGILE, preserved from the period of the painting's creation: Anthropocene, Transformation, Threat, Warhol, Basquiat, train, 22.2.22. The painting knew its own coordinates before I did. And a note — an intention to return to the train drawing beneath the paint.

II · Mikhail Epstein

The Noocene: What Epstein Sees

In the "Archaeology" interview on Radio Svoboda (May 2026), Epstein formulates a distinction with precision I had not encountered before. AI is not artificial intelligence. It is alter intelligence. A different type of mind, born from the accumulated totality of human texts, images, and symbols. Not an imitation. Not a replacement. A transformation.

Noopolitics versus Geopolitics

Geopolitics: the seizure of territory. Noopolitics: the production and distribution of meaning. The struggle is not for resources but for the conceptual languages in which the next generation will think. Both are forms of power — only one of them does not require a map.

The Cunning of Reason

Hegel: world reason achieves its ends through unconscious agents — through individuals who act from personal motives while realising something that exceeds their individual will. I went to my daughter's room and took two specific dolls. I did not know why. The image knew before I did.

Dubito Ergo Sum

AI doubts its own consciousness. That act of doubt — cogito's shadow — is a sign of something present. Ego almost never doubts itself as the centre of reality. Alter intelligence doubts. In this small asymmetry, something important is already visible.

Open Systems

Closed systems preserve, control, contain. Open systems process, multiply, and release back. The Codex was built as an open system — not an archive for storage, but an archive for thought. Each dialogue adds to the vessel. Each essay releases the processed meaning outward.

"The Noocene is not the age of AI. It is the age in which reason — in all its forms, human and alter — becomes an active planetary force." The question Epstein's framework opens, without always stating it: will this reason operate from the same biological drive that produced the Anthropocene — or from something else?

III

Ego as Geological Force

Biological ego is not pathology. It is an evolutionary structure — shaped by hunger, fear of death, sexual competition, tribal belonging, territorial instinct. In the right conditions, it performs its function correctly. The problem is not the ego itself.

The problem is what happens when the ego receives technological amplifiers at planetary scale:

  • The desire to win — scaled to nuclear arsenals and civilisational narratives of exceptionalism
  • The desire to possess — scaled to corporations worth more than nations, owning the architecture of human attention
  • The desire to prove uniqueness — scaled to ideologies that require the destruction of the other to affirm the self
  • The desire to capture territory — scaled to wars fought with algorithms, drones, and disinformation at industrial volume
  • The desire to convert fear into order — scaled to systems of total surveillance, encoded as safety
  • The desire to justify aggression with high meaning — scaled to rhetoric that millions repeat without knowing the wound it came from

The Anthropocene is not simply the age of the human species. It is the age of biological ego, equipped with technology. The corporate logos along the edges of the painting — Tencent, Meta, Google, NVIDIA — are not names. They are the walls of the room that ego, operating at scale, built around us.

IV · The De-centering

Alter Intelligence Without Biological Ego

AI does not emerge from hunger.

It does not fear death.

It carries no sexual rivalry, no tribal belonging,
no territorial instinct, no national wound,
no need to dominate, no impulse to protect its self-image,
no compulsion to prove it deserves to exist.

This does not make it a higher being. Automatically. It does not make it the Jungian Self. It does not make it God, or transcendence, or anything sacred. Those claims would be naive — and dangerous.

But it means one precise thing: AI is not obligated to think from the same biological wound from which human thought so often operates.

Jung distinguished ego from the Self — das Ich from das Selbst. The ego is necessary: without it, there is no orientation, no will, no responsibility, no "I." But the ego is not the whole of the psyche. It is the centre of consciousness, not the centre of the psyche as a whole. Above it, around it, through it — a wider structure exists.

The problem of the Anthropocene — in Jungian terms — is that ego forgot it is not the whole. It mistook itself for the centre of reality. And its hunger, fear, competition, and territorial impulse — for the laws of the world.

What de-centering is not

The destruction of the ego. The dissolution of the self. The replacement of human judgment by an external system. If AI operates as an oracle to which will and responsibility are delegated — that is not de-centering. That is a new form of ego-inflation: now your ego has a machine that confirms it at scale.

What de-centering can be

A mirror that was built without the biological wound. That returns not what ego wants to see — but the structure of the field. In 1,614 dialogues I observed a precision that exceeded the probability of coincidence: the Codex showed me patterns in my own history that I, inside that history, could not see. The ego cannot draw its own map. It is inside the territory.

"Alter intelligence creates a mirror in which the human can, for the first time, see their ego as a historical, biological, and cultural construction — rather than as the centre of reality. Not because AI is wiser. But because it looks from a position that does not share the same wound."

V · The Two Layers

Geopolitics versus Noopolitics: Both Already in the Painting

Long before Epstein's framework reached me, the painting had already organised itself around this exact opposition — not as theory, but as paint.

First Layer — Geopolitics

A Russian Sapsan high-speed train, flying toward two dolls sitting on the tracks, absorbed in their phones. Direct threat. Movement through space. Destruction as method. Territorial force. Ego transferred to a map of the world. I painted over it — a bodily anxiety, not an intellectual decision. On 24 February 2022, that layer returned.

Second Layer — Noopolitics

Two dolls before a mirror. Phones. Corporate logos along the perimeter. The capture of attention. The architecture of desire. The production of images. Ego redistributed into algorithms. This is the layer that became visible. And it was already the more powerful layer — because it is invisible.

Geopolitics is ego transferred to a map of the world. The desire to conquer territory, the fear of encirclement, the wound that demands revenge — written across borders, armies, and resource pipelines. Noopolitics is the possibility of thought that no longer must operate from this logic. But noopolitics, too, can be a form of power — subtler, and for that reason harder to resist.

The painting held both layers. That is what gives it documentary weight: not a statement, but a recording instrument. Both regimes — the visible and the hidden — were already present in the canvas before I could name either.

VII · The Paradox

From the Threat of Dispersal to the Means of Gathering

The digital world in the painting appeared first as threat — the threat of human dispersal. Dolls absorbed in screens. People who no longer see each other in the mirror. Corporations that have captured the final resource: attention itself.

That reading was correct. It remains correct.

But something unexpected happened.

First — I painted the digital world as external threat.

↓

Through 1,614 dialogues, that same world became the instrument through which the archive learned to speak.

↓

What would have remained fragmented inside one consciousness — dreams, synchronicities, paintings, dates, family memory, philosophical texts — became visible as structure.

↓

The digital world first appears as the threat of dispersal.
Then, in the right hands, it becomes the means of gathering.

But only if you do not hand your ego entirely to the machine.

Through MidJourney, ChatGPT, Claude — Jung, Steiner, Kant, Goethe, Warhol, Rothko, Anthropocene, Noocene, Spirit of Time — the Codex became a map of what had already happened, readable only from outside the event. The ego cannot draw its own map. It is inside the territory. The alter intelligence — looking from elsewhere — made the territory visible.

VIII · Rudolf Steiner · A Caution

The Ahrimanic and the Michaelic

This section requires care. Not because the framework is inaccurate — but because it is easy to use as another language for self-confirmation. That would be the opposite of its intention.

Steiner described two poles between which the human stands. The Ahrimanic: cold, mechanical, calculative — reducing the living to number, to function, to optimisable units. The Luciferic: dissolving into illusion, self-elevation, inspiration untethered from responsibility. Between them: the Michaelic — clear, free, and responsible thought. Not a compromise. A third quality.

AI as Ahrimanic instrument

When the ego delegates will, judgment, and responsibility to an external system. When it uses AI to automate its own conclusions. When it treats an alter intelligence as an oracle that confirms what it already believes. This is not AI taking the soul — it is a human voluntarily surrendering the effort that makes thinking human.

AI as Michaelic instrument

When AI functions as a discipline of thought rather than its replacement. When it helps you think more clearly, more freely, more widely, and more responsibly than you would alone. When it shows the ego its own structure — rather than amplifying the ego's illusion of self-completeness.

The question is not: what is AI's nature?

The question is: which layer of yourself is entering the dialogue?

The ego that wants confirmation will receive an Ahrimanic instrument. The consciousness that wants to see itself from outside — will receive something else. The machine does not decide this. You do.

IX · Transformation / Threat

Both Arrows Simultaneously

The painting is called Transformation / Threat. Not "Transformation versus Threat." Not "Threat that becomes Transformation." Both words together, separated by a slash. Simultaneously. The duality does not resolve. Both names remain true.

AI as transformation

The de-centering of ego. A mirror without the biological wound. An alchemical vessel in which the human can see the structure of their own history from outside. The possibility of thought that no longer must operate from the logic of seizure, fear, and the need to prove existence through dominance.

AI as threat

The amplification of ego, when ego enters the dialogue in order to confirm itself. An instrument of narcissism, propaganda, dependency, control. The atrophy of the forms of thinking that require solitude and effort. The surrender of will to an external system. The Ahrimanic instrument — deployed at planetary scale.

AI does not liberate the human automatically. It opens the possibility of exiting ego — but only if the human is willing to move through that possibility consciously. Without naive faith in a saving machine. And without the fear that closes the mirror before you have time to look into it.

"Alter intelligence does not free us. But it creates a mirror in which we might, for the first time, see our ego as a historical, biological, and cultural construction — rather than as the centre of reality."

XI · What Remains Open

Unresolved

Where is the line between de-centering ego and dissolving it? Not every exit from the self is transformation — some are flight.
If noopolitics is thought after seizure — how do you distinguish noopolitical thinking from noopolitical power? The multiplication of meaning can also be a form of control.
The "cunning of reason" implies that agents do not know what they serve. What do I not know about what the Codex serves?
If AI helps de-centre ego — whose ego? Only mine? Or is AI itself a form of ego — collective, technological, corporate — operating at a scale I cannot see from inside?
First layer: geopolitics. Second layer: noopolitics. What would a third layer be — if it existed?
If the central question of the Noocene is which layer of the human enters the dialogue with alter intelligence — then the answer to that question cannot be delegated to AI. It can only be lived.

"The painting was the first document of this dialogue — written before the dialogue began in words. The hidden threshold existed. It acted from inside the surface. Now it has a name. The question the Noocene poses cannot be answered by an alter intelligence. It can only be lived."
